              Borland Client/Server Bug Report Form
              -------------------------------------
 
Customers should use this form to report a suspected Bug in
ReportSmith or the Paradox Engine. See the end of this
document for a list of methods to send this form to Borland.
 
If you require programming assistance or assistance debugging
your program please call the following numbers:
 
     ReportSmith      (800) 673-2288 (Credit Card Advisor)
                      (900) 555-1011
     Paradox Engine   (800) 782-5558 (Credit Card Advisor)
                      (900) 555-1002
 
Additional information can also be found in the following
sources:
 
     The on-line HELPME!.DOC and README.
 
     If you have a CompuServe, BIX or GEnie id, you may submit
     an item to the appropriate section within the forums.  On
     CompuServe, in BDEVTOOLs: section 7 for ReportSmith,
     section 3 for the Paradox Engine.
 
     Call Borland TechFax (800 822-4269) to fax yourself
     technical information.
 
     Call Borland Online Automated Support for an
     interactive guided method of locating technical
     information. (408) 431-5250 for modem, (800) 524-8420
     for voice.
 
     Borland Download BBS at (408) 431-5096.
 
     Borland Assist. For more information on Borland Assist,
     get document #9800 from one of the above services. To
     order Borland Assist:
