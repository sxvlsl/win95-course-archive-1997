    TITLE  :  Selections on Paradox ODBC Date Fields




                                               May 19, 1994

Paradox Date Fix

ReportSmith v2.02a

This information corrects an error generated by the ODBC driver 
when querying against a Paradox date field.  The following 
lines should be placed in the [Q+E ParadoxFile] section of the 
RS_SQLIF.INI file which is found in the C:\WINDOWS directory.

DateFormat= " "{d ' "yyyy-mm-dd" '}" "
TimeFormat= " "{t ' "hh:mm:ss" '}" "
DateTimeFormat= " "{ts ' "yyyy-mm-dd hh:mm:ss.ssssss" '}" "
