    TITLE  :  Date Calculation




Date Calculation
 
The following macro example shows how to add days to a date field.
 
 
Sub add2date()
 
'Return a Julian Date
Jdate = Str2Date(Date$,1)
 
'Add 21 Days
Plus21Days = Jdate + 21
 
'Convert the date back to a string
NewDate$ = Date2Str$(Plus21Days,3)
 
DerivedField NewDate$
 
End Sub
