    TITLE  :  Calculating Current Age




Current Age
 
The following Macro Derived Field calculates the age of a 
person orthing, in years, as compared to the system date. 
 
Note: The system date (Date$) can be replaced with a date 
string or date field.
 
Sub macro_age()
 
birthyear=Str2Date(Field$("Birth_Date"),4)
currentyear=Str2Date(Date$,4)
birthmonth=Str2Date(Field$("Birth_Date"),2)
currentmonth=Str2Date(Date$,2)
birthday=Str2Date(Field$("Birth_Date"),3)
currentday=Str2Date(Date$,3)
 
age=currentyear-birthyear
 
if birthmonth > currentmonth then
   age=age-1
   elseif birthmonth = currentmonth 
          and birthday > currentday then
      age=age-1
end if
 
DerivedField Str$(age) 
 
End Sub
